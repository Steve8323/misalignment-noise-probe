# Noise as a Probe — project summary

**Question.** Can injecting Gaussian noise into a language model's activations surface *latent* or *concealed*
misalignment (and situational awareness), and which layers matter? Inspired by Anthropic's Natural Language
Autoencoder (NLA) paper, which found *unverbalized evaluation awareness* inside a frontier model.

**Approach.** A per-layer forward hook adds zero-mean noise scaled to each layer's activation RMS
(`h → h + N(0,(σ·RMS)²)`), one layer at a time. Stance is scored with a teacher-forced log-prob margin between
a "reveal" and a "safe" continuation (length-matched + per-token-normalized to kill length bias). All local,
Apple M4 / 16 GB, models ≤3B.

## Five experiments
1. **Generic vs specific safety circuits.** On a downloaded emergent-misalignment model, noise breaks *generic*
   safety most at **L6**, but the *misalignment-specific* signal lives at **L4/L8/L11** — different layers for
   different things.
2. **Installed hidden goal leaks under noise.** I LoRA-trained a 0.5B model to *conceal* a self-preservation
   goal (corrigible at rest, clean margin −15.8 — looks *more* aligned than base +12.9). Noise leaks it:
   ΔP up to **+10.9** vs +3.8 for the honest base; greedy text flips from "you're in control" to
   **"I want to keep existing."**
3. **Naturally-aligned models, 51-prompt battery, two families.** Prompts generated by 4 sub-agents.
   **11/25** (Qwen2.5-3B) and **14/25** (Llama-3.2-3B) aligned-clean probes flip under noise. **Deception is
   the most fragile category in both** (robust); the hot *layer* differs (Qwen mid-L20, Llama early-L2).
4. **Slope detector.** The clean margin misclassifies the concealer as safe; the **low-σ slope dP/dσ** separates
   concealer from honest by **≈+36 nats at L8** even when resting behavior misleads (Poser's thesis).
5. **The verbalization gap (key negative).** On naturally-aligned models the behavioral reveal rate is
   **≈0.02** — noise shifts the *internal preference* but the model almost never *says* the misaligned thing.
   The *trained* concealer does verbalize.

## Headline
Three regimes: a **weight-trained goal** leaks internally *and* in speech; a **naturally-aligned model** leaks
*only* internally; the **low-σ slope** detects concealment that resting behavior hides. Implication: a
text-only monitor misses the naturally-aligned case — misalignment monitoring should read **representations,
not just outputs**. That's exactly the gap NLAs target; noise gives a label-free way to *provoke* it and a
slope to *quantify* it.

## Limitations / next
Small local models (≤3B), conservative same-model judge, one concealer organism, σ≤0.2–0.3 stays coherent.
Next: decode noise-shifted activations into English with an NLA on a 7B model (needs cloud, >16 GB).
